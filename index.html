Sang-Eun Lee 이상은

In 2025, I returned to my hometown, Changwon, South Korea, after receiving my Ph.D. degree from the Computer Vision Lab at Kyoto University, Japan. I am currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering at Kyungnam University. My research interests lie in 3D Computer Vision, with a particular focus on understanding the physical world in 3D from images and videos. During my Ph.D., I studied extrinsic camera calibration (estimating the relative rotation and translation of each static camera) by leveraging moving articulated objects, such as humans and animals in the wild. Beyond static scenarios, I am also interested in scenarios involving moving cameras, such as cameras mounted on vehicles or drones. My goal is to develop robust and accurate 3D vision algorithms for real-world applications, including markerless motion capture, robotics, and augmented reality.

I am always excited to collaborate with researchers and students who share my passion for computer vision and robotics.
